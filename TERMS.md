## What PhaseJumper is

PhaseJumper is a free, open-source planner. It turns your flights and your usual sleep times into a day-by-day plan for light, sleep, caffeine and other habits, based on published sleep and body-clock research. It runs in your browser; there is no account and no server.

## General wellness information, not medical advice

- PhaseJumper gives general wellness information. It is not medical advice and it is not a substitute for a clinician.
- If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or take medication, talk to a clinician before changing your sleep, light, caffeine, exercise or melatonin habits.
- The plan describes what research suggests about timing. It does not tell you what you must do, and it never suggests an amount or a product.
