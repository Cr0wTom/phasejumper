# Privacy notice

Version 2, 13 October 2026.

## The short version

PhaseJumper does not collect, receive or store any personal data, unless you choose to email feedback (see below). There are no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking and no server of our own. Everything you type stays in your browser.

## What stays on your device

Your browser keeps the following, only on this device:

- your trips and plans, in the browser's site storage (IndexedDB);
- your light or dark theme choice, in the browser's local storage;
- the version of the terms you accepted and when, in the browser's local storage.

None of this is sent anywhere. To delete it, use **Settings → Forget all trips**, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## Files and links you create

- **Share links** contain your trip (flights, usual sleep times and the choices you made) inside the part of the link after the "#". Browsers do not send that part to the website host, so it never reaches us or the host. Anyone you give the link to can see that trip.
- **Calendar files and plan exports** are saved to your device. If you import them into a calendar or another service, that service's own terms and privacy notice apply.
- **Technical report:** if you choose "Copy technical report" in Settings, PhaseJumper copies a short technical summary to your clipboard: the app and planner versions, your browser's user agent, the build's feature switches, a one-way fingerprint of your trip (not the trip itself) and any planner warnings. It is only shared if you paste it somewhere yourself.

## Feedback by email

Settings has links to report a bug or request a feature. They open your own email app with a message addressed to feedback@phasejumper.world; nothing is sent until you press send there. A bug report message starts with the technical report described above, which you can read and delete before sending.

If you send feedback, the maintainer receives what is in your email: your email address, your name if your email app adds it, and what you wrote. It is used only to read and answer your feedback and to fix or plan the app, it is never shared or sold, and it is deleted within 12 months of the conversation ending. You can ask for a copy of it, its correction or its deletion at any time by writing to the same address. The maintainer is responsible for this data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, and you may complain to a data protection authority.

## The hosting provider

PhaseJumper is a set of static files served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when your browser loads the site, such as your IP address, and handles it under its own privacy statement: [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not receive this information.

## Links to other sites

The research page links to articles on other websites. Those sites are contacted only if you open a link, and their own privacy notices apply.

## Features that are switched off

The code contains switches for two possible future features: a live flight-status lookup and opt-in error reporting. Both are off in this version and send nothing. If either is ever switched on, this notice will be updated first and PhaseJumper will ask you to accept the new version.

## Your rights

Apart from feedback you email, no personal data reaches us: you hold all of it on your device and can delete it at any time as described above. For feedback emails, see "Feedback by email". For your data held by GitHub as the host, see GitHub's privacy statement.

## Questions

Write to feedback@phasejumper.world, or open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
